# My ARL demo

A clickable demo of **My ARL**, the player and game-night app for the Adult Recreational League.

**Open it:** https://lusky3.github.io/my-arl-demo/

Everything here is sample data. The teams are real ARL team names; the players, games and scores are made up. Nothing you tap is saved or sent anywhere, and reloading the page starts over.

Use the bar at the top to see the app as a **Player**, a **Spare** or an **Organizer**.

The app itself lives in the `sportspress-player-app` plugin of SportsPress Admin Tools.
